The art of the flip

A few simple rules. Endless little decisions.

  1. 01

    Find your opening

    Place a stone on an empty square. It must trap at least one opposing stone between your new stone and another of your own.

  2. 02

    Make it your color

    Every trapped stone flips to your color. Look along rows, columns, and diagonals; one move can flip several lines.

  3. 03

    Keep the majority

    No legal move? Your turn passes. When neither player can move, the game ends. The player with the most stones wins.

Make the board your own. Choose a 6×6, 8×8, or 10×10 board, stone or wood, and your favorite disc colors in the game’s settings.
